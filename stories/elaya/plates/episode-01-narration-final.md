# Episode 1 — Narration, final

The merge of v1 and v2. **Not yet recorded** — 1.52 credits on the balance,
~6 needed.

## What each version contributed

**Kept from v1** — the writing. v2 traded these away for concision and the trade
was bad:

- *"for a hotel that expected forty people and got them, briefly"*
- *"nobody asked him which — he's been dead eleven years"*
- The aunt rubbing the tab blank over years and never saying why
- **The four rings.** v2 compressed *"Twice by mistake. Once by a child. And once
  by a woman who was already dying and wanted the window opened"* down to "four
  times in thirty years." That is the best line in the episode and cutting it was
  the single worst decision in v2.
- *"She left no note. She never does."*

**Kept from v2** — the structure and the two things v1 was missing:

- Elaya introduced in the first line, so the audience knows who they are watching
- The 3am doorway given its own line instead of playing under bell trivia
- Warmth: contractions, a dry aside, the rhythm of someone describing a person
  they know rather than reading an inventory

## Timing

**211 words.** v1 ran 170 words in 59.4s — about 2.9 words per second — so this
lands near **74 seconds** against 65 seconds of picture.

Rather than cut, **hold two shots longer in the edit**:

- **Clip 6, the tab drops: +4s.** The best line in the episode plays over it and
  currently has five seconds. The swinging tab coming to rest wants the time
  anyway.
- **Clip 13, the shut door: +5s.** The closing image improves with silence.

That makes 74 seconds of picture for 74 seconds of voice. No trimming, and both
holds are improvements in their own right.

## Two notes for the read

**Clip 6** carries the episode's best writing and must not be performed. Four
facts in a row, the last one longer than the others, and no change of tone on the
dying woman. The line does the work; the voice should stay out of its way.

**Clip 9** is read at exactly the same temperature as clip 6. If the delivery
turns ominous there, the whole design breaks — the point is that she finds this
unremarkable.

## Silence

Two deliberate gaps: clip 7, where the picture explains itself, and clips 12-13 at
the close. The episode ends on an object, not a sentence.
