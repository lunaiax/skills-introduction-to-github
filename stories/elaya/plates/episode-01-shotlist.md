## Not yet shot

**The 3am doorway.** Elaya standing in the doorway of room four looking at Havel
asleep, before she takes his things. The single most important image in the
episode and the hardest — it needs Havel, and it has to read as *odd* rather than
sinister. Deliberately held until the tone is settled.

## Why three shots need re-rendering

Shots 4, 5 and 7 were rendered before the architecture decision. They carry v2's
**white pilasters and contrasting trim**, which is now explicitly wrong — the
building is pink on pink with no white anywhere. Interiors are less affected than
exteriors, but the hall in particular is likely to show white mouldings against
the pink.

Shot 3 is probably safe: the kitchen is cream and sage green by spec, so it never
depended on the white trim. Shot 6 is safe by definition — the linen room breaks
the house style on purpose.
