## Why the interiors were re-shot

Two errors in the blocks, not in the model:

1. **"Art Nouveau mouldings" was written into every interior.** Art Nouveau is
   the opposite of baroque — sinuous organic whiplash instead of gilded cartouche,
   acanthus and putti. The renders were correct to the prompt and the prompt was
   wrong. Interiors are now baroque; the exterior stays Jugendstil.
2. **Pink-on-pink was applied indoors.** That rule belongs to the *exterior*. Every
   interior in the reference set is polychrome — mint and yellow and pink, sage and
   cream, pink and blue. The monochrome rule was suppressing the colour. Interiors
   now follow the per-room assignments in `art-direction.md` §3A.

## The shape of the run

Wide, close, wide, wide, tight, **square**, wide.

Each interior owns a different colour, so the run reads as walking through a box
of different sweets: green kitchen, pink hall, raspberry dining room, yellow
bedroom, blue corridor — with the linen room as the one room with no colour and no
beauty in it.

## Judging shot 6

The frame is deliberately pretty: symmetrical, warm, evenly lit, her expression
calm and ordinary, nothing sinister in the lighting. The wrongness must come from
the situation, not the photography. If it reads as menacing the lighting has
overcooked it; if it reads as charming the situation is not landing.
