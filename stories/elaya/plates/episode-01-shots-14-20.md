## 14 · The symbolic funeral

Where it goes: early, probably second or third, before the hotel is established.
It explains why she is alone in a building this size.

Not a funeral with mourners — a funeral with almost nobody, which is the point.

**Still prompt**

```text
<<<69639e42-554b-41a5-a8a2-37589d96b5d4>>> stands entirely alone at the centre of
a vast baroque hotel ballroom, holding a single white lily. She wears a
butter-cream gown with enormous puffed sleeves, a powder-blue ribbon bow at the
throat, and dusty pink opera gloves to the elbow. In front of her, one gilt chair
faces the empty room with a folded grey shawl laid across its seat and a small
framed photograph propped against it. Every other chair in the ballroom is
stacked against the walls.

The ballroom is shell pink and gold: pink marble pilasters with gilded Corinthian
capitals, heavily carved gilded plasterwork, tall mirrored panels in ornate gilt
frames, three immense crystal chandeliers, a painted ceiling of sky and clouds,
and a parquet floor. Nobody else is present. Tall windows throw clean daylight
across the empty floor.

Style: opulent baroque interior, Wes Anderson confectionery maximalism, richly
saturated colour, shot on Kodak Ektachrome, crisp and clean, photographic.
Perfectly symmetrical flat frontal composition, dead-centre vanishing point,
wide-angle lens with slight barrel distortion. Deep focus. Format: 16:9.
```

**Motion — crane up, 4s.** Start close on the lily in her gloved hands, rise and
pull back until she is small at the centre of an enormous empty room. The move
*is* the idea: one person, one chair, four hundred square metres.

## 15 · The garden, upper terrace

**Still prompt**

```text
A grand formal terraced garden below a pink Jugendstil palace hotel, photographed
straight on down the central axis. Clipped box parterres in perfect symmetry,
gravel walks, standard roses in pink stone urns, a long reflecting canal on the
centre line, and stone steps descending between low balustrades toward a
wildflower meadow. The pink hotel rises behind; grey mountains beyond under a pale
clear sky. Immaculately kept and completely empty of people.

Style: photographic, richly saturated pastels, shot on Kodak Ektachrome, crisp and
clean, real light and real material. Bright clean daylight. Perfectly symmetrical
flat frontal composition, dead-centre vanishing point, wide-angle lens with slight
barrel distortion. Deep focus. Format: 16:9.
```

**Motion — fast dolly forward down the canal, 3s.** Straight, quick, symmetry
holding all the way. Lands square.

## A note on shot 20

This is the first time in the episode Elaya and a guest **look at each other**.
Everything before it is her watching people who don't know they're watched. Placed
after the errand, it changes what the errand meant — she does the anonymous thing
*and* she can talk to him perfectly well. She isn't incapable. She chooses it.

Worth deciding whether that belongs in episode 1 at all, or whether it's stronger
held for later. My instinct is it belongs — the audience needs one glimpse of her
functioning normally, or she's a ghost rather than a person.
