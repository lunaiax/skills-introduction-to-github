## Tobin — the postmaster

Forties. Cold, still, does not gesture. Carries things up hills. The same grey
wool coat in every season, removed indoors with a formality nobody comments on.
Children are slightly afraid of him and he has never tried to fix it.

The look to avoid: handsome-and-brooding. He should read as **plain, watchful and
slightly wrong** — a man you would not look at twice until you noticed he had
been looking at you.
