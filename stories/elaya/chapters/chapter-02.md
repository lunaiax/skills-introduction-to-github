# Episode 2: The Wrong Yellow

## Logline

The village repaints the bus shelter a yellow that is almost the villa's, and Elaya cannot stop looking at it. She secretly repaints it at night, correctly.

## Intent

First full errand, comic and small. Establishes that she acts at night and never signs her work.

## Outline

2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.
