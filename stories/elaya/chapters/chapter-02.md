# Episode 2: The Suitcase That Was Never Opened

## Logline

A young woman books two weeks, pays cash, never unpacks, and walks to the bus
shelter every afternoon to watch a bus she does not board. Elaya has fourteen days
to work out what is in the suitcase.

## The Guests

**Mila Vesth**, 23, sunburnt, self-cut hair, no coat, one locked leather case.
**Hanno Krebs**, 28, still wearing his wedding suit nine days later.

## The Case

*The clues, in the order Elaya collects them:*

1. Cash in advance — nobody has done that since 1958.
2. Three days of clothes, washed nightly in the basin, for a fourteen-day stay.
3. A man's pullover that does not fit her.
4. The case is good, brass-locked, and light. Whatever is in it is bulky and
   weighs nothing.
5. Ten past four, every day, the shelter. She watches the bus leave.
6. On the register she writes her surname, stops, and writes it again over the
   top, slightly differently.

**Solution:** the case holds a wedding dress worn once for forty minutes. Mila
was not left. Mila left — at the door — and cannot go forward or back, because
opening the case makes it real and boarding the bus makes it permanent.

Then, on day nine, the groom arrives. He is not angry. That is the problem.

## The Errand

Elaya picks the lock at two in the morning, takes the dress, and has Doro cut it
down over four nights into a plain travelling coat in cream wool. It goes back
into the locked case without a note.

On day fifteen Mila opens the case for the first time, and wears the coat, and
boards.

## What Elaya Withholds

She never tells Hanno that Mila is in the building. She gives him one true fact
instead — that the ten-past-four is the only bus out of the valley — and lets him
work it out three days late.

The series does not say whether that was mercy or cowardice.

## Unease

At three in the morning Peter Lind, eleven, out in pyjamas under a man's coat,
watches a woman in a cream ball gown and pink gloves kneeling at the bus shelter
with a needle in her mouth.

He tells everyone. Nobody believes him. **Except Tobin**, who does not defend the
boy, but writes down what he said, with the date, and puts it in a drawer.
