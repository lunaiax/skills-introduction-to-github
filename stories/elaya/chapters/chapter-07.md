# Episode 7: What Elaya Does Not Do

## Logline

Tobin waits, plainly, for a sentence. Elaya arranges a beautiful evening for two other people instead and goes home alone.

## Intent

The cowardice episode. Everything she is good at, deployed precisely to avoid herself. Should be almost unbearable.

## Outline

1. Cold open — an object, examined. The narrator names it before anyone speaks.
2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.
