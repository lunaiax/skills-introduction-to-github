# Episode 3: A Letter Not Addressed to Her

## Logline

Elaya reads a guest's abandoned letter, learns of a debt the writer is too ashamed to name, and quietly settles it in a way that looks like luck.

## Intent

Raises the ethics: she is helping and she is trespassing, and the episode refuses to resolve which matters more.

## Outline

1. Cold open — an object, examined. The narrator names it before anyone speaks.
2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.
