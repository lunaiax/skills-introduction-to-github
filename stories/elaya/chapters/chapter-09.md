# Episode 9: Someone Asks

## Logline

Tobin asks about the gloves. Directly, kindly, once. Elaya leaves the room. The village, meanwhile, quietly repaints a shutter for her.

## Intent

The whole series inverts: for the first time, the errand is done FOR her, anonymously, in her own method.

## Outline

1. Cold open — an object, examined. The narrator names it before anyone speaks.
2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.

## Series Thread

She learns what it feels like from the other side
