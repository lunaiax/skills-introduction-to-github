# Episode 1: The One Bell That Rings

## Logline

A guest rings a bell nobody can place. Elaya spends the episode hunting the room it belongs to and, finding it, finds her aunt's things exactly as left.

## Intent

Establish the villa's scale, Elaya's method, and the gloves as an unremarked fact. Ends on the locked room she does not open.

## Outline

1. Cold open — an object, examined. The narrator names it before anyone speaks.
2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.

## Script

**EPISODE ONE — "THE ONE BELL THAT RINGS"**

---

`FADE IN:`

**INT. VILLA MAREAU — KITCHEN — DAY**

Close on a BELL BOARD mounted high on the wall. Twenty-two brass tabs in
two rows, each with a room number engraved beneath it. The brass is dull.
Nothing moves.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> The board was fitted in 1911, for a house that expected forty people and
> got them, briefly.

A hand in a PINK OPERA GLOVE enters frame and dusts the board, tab by tab,
without hurrying.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Twenty-two bells. Twenty-one of them were disconnected in 1963, when the
> wiring was done properly for the first and last time.

The glove stops at one tab. Number is worn away.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> The electrician kept one. Nobody asked him which, and he has been dead
> eleven years.

**WIDE.** The kitchen is enormous and holds one person. ELAYA, mid-twenties,
in a butter-cream gown with sleeves too large for any kitchen, lowers her
hand and looks at the board the way other people look at weather.

---

**EXT. THE MEADOW GATE — DAY**

The wrought-iron gates stand open in a wildflower meadow. Rust-brown, scrolled,
grass grown through the bars. There is no wall on either side of them.

A GUEST — sixties, good coat, bad shoes for this — walks up the track. He
reaches the gates. He could pass either side of them in three steps.

He walks through them.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Everyone does.

---

**INT. VILLA MAREAU — ENTRANCE HALL — CONTINUOUS**

A double staircase built for a crowd. The guest stands at the bottom of it,
small. Elaya comes down. She does not hurry for guests either.

> **ELAYA**
> You'll want four. It's the only one that's warm.

> **GUEST**
> I asked for eleven.

> **ELAYA**
> You did.

She takes his bag. He does not let go of it at first, then does.

> **GUEST**
> Is there someone I should speak to? The owner.

Elaya turns, already climbing.

> **ELAYA**
> There isn't, really.

`CUT TO:`

---

**INT. VILLA MAREAU — KITCHEN — NIGHT**

Elaya alone, counting cutlery under her breath — *four, five, six* — laying
a table in the great dining room for three.

Behind her, on the wall, ONE BRASS TAB DROPS.

A bell rings. Thin, small, absolutely clear.

Elaya stops counting.

She turns. Looks at the board. The tab that has fallen is the worn one — the
one with no number.

It rings again.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> It had been rung four times in thirty years. Twice by mistake. Once by a
> child. And once, on a Tuesday in March, by a woman who was already dying
> and wanted the window opened.

Elaya crosses to the board. Her gloved hand hovers, not touching.

The bell rings a third time.

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

**MAIN TITLES.**

---

*(End of cold open — approximately 2 pages. Full episode continues: Elaya
walks the twenty-two rooms in order, testing each bell pull, and the hunt
carries her to the one door in the house she has not opened since the funeral.)*
