# Episode 5: Eleven Years of Not Speaking

## Logline

Two brothers in the village have not spoken since their mother's funeral. Elaya engineers a reconciliation using a broken clock and a lie of omission.

## Intent

The high-water mark of her powers, and the first time an errand nearly fails. Midpoint: she is caught, by Tobin, and he says nothing.

## Outline

1. Cold open — an object, examined. The narrator names it before anyone speaks.
2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.

## Series Thread

Tobin knows; chooses not to expose her
