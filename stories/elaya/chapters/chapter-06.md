# Episode 6: The Guest Who Cries in the Morning

## Logline

A woman comes to the villa to decide whether to leave her husband. Elaya, for once, does nothing — and the woman decides anyway.

## Intent

The counter-episode. Elaya learns her interventions were never the mechanism. Destabilises her whole self-image.

## Outline

1. Cold open — an object, examined. The narrator names it before anyone speaks.
2. The errand presents itself.
3. Elaya observes, decides, and acts sideways.
4. The errand lands on someone who does not know its source.
5. Button — one shot through the meadow gate.

## Series Thread

Turn: her method is called into question
