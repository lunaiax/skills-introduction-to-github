# The Small Errands of Elaya

## Synopsis

A gloved young woman quietly rearranges the lives of everyone who passes through her late aunt's alpine villa, while never once arranging her own.

## Tone & Style

Amelie is the reference, but the palette is the argument with it. Amelie is
saturated red and green and moves fast. This is butter-cream, powder blue, rust
and wet meadow-white, and it moves slowly. Same engine, opposite temperature: a
whimsical fixer of other people's lives who cannot touch her own, told in a house
too big for her and a valley that has already decided who she is.

Rules the series keeps:

- **The narrator names objects, never feelings.** "The bell was brass. It had been
  rung four times in thirty years." Never "she felt alone."
- **Every errand is anonymous.** Elaya never once receives thanks on screen until
  episode 9, when it is done to her instead.
- **The gloves are never explained early.** Every episode may look at them. No
  episode before 10 may account for them.
- **One shot through the meadow gate per episode.** In episode 10, somebody walks
  around it.
- **No swelling music at the end.** The endings are small on purpose.

## Format

Prose, not screenplay. Ten episodes as prose chapters, present tense, third
limited on Elaya with a narrator who stands slightly outside her.

The narrator is the engine and needs a firm rule: **it states facts about objects
and withholds facts about feelings.** "That bell has been rung four times in thirty
years" is the register. "She felt alone in the big house" is not, ever. The reader
does the feeling; the narrator does the inventory. Where the narrator states an
arithmetic Elaya is also doing, it may say so.

## Structure

Ten episodes. Episodic on the surface — one errand per episode — with a single
throughline underneath: Elaya's own life, which she is the only person in the
valley not attending to.

- **1-4 Establish.** The house, the method, the village, and Tobin.
- **5-6 Test.** Her powers peak, then the ground moves: episode 6 shows an errand
  she does not perform and an outcome that happens anyway.
- **7-8 Cost.** What the method is actually protecting her from.
- **9-10 Inversion and payoff.** The village does an errand for her. Then she answers.
