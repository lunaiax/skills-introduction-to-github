# The Bell Board

## Description

Fitted 1911. Twenty-two small brass tabs in two rows in a glazed dark-wood case,
each with a room number engraved in copperplate beneath it. Mounted high on the
kitchen wall.

Twenty-one bells were disconnected in 1963 when the wiring was done properly for
the first and last time. The electrician kept one live. Nobody asked him which,
and he has been dead eleven years.

**Second row, seventh from the left**, the tab has had its number rubbed smooth
and blank — patiently, over years, by Aunt Mareau. The engraving is still there
under the blank; Elaya can feel the grooves through her glove.

The live bell answers Room Eleven.

## Why It Matters

The series' most-repeated object and its quiet clock. It must be rendered
identically every time: 22 tabs, two rows, the blank one second row seventh from
the left.
