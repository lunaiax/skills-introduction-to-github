# The Pool Terrace

## Description

A rectangular pool on a red-and-cream checkerboard deck, rows of folded cream
parasols, coral loungers, clipped topiary in gold urns. The hotel rises behind;
mountains beyond.

The water is heated, filtered and completely still.

## Why It Matters

The most expensive thing in the hotel to maintain and the least used. Elaya keeps
it perfect. In episode 6 the guest who is deciding whether to leave her husband
swims in it alone, and it is the only time anyone does.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.10. Still water, folded parasols, empty loungers, dead
symmetry. Episode 6 is the exception and should feel like one.
