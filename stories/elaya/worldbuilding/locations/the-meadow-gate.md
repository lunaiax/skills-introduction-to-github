# The Meadow Gate

## Description

A pair of wrought-iron gates, rust-brown, scrolled at the top, standing open in a
wildflower meadow below the villa. They have stood open so long the grass has grown
through them, and there is no fence on either side — the wall they belonged to came
down decades ago.

So they gate nothing. You can walk around them in three steps. Everybody walks
through them anyway.

## Why It Matters

This is the series' central image and its thesis in one object: a threshold that
stops nothing and that everyone honours regardless. Elaya's whole character is the
gate — a barrier with no wall behind it, kept because taking it down was never
considered.

## Rules For The Camera

Every episode contains at least one shot through the gates. In episode 10 somebody
walks around them instead.
