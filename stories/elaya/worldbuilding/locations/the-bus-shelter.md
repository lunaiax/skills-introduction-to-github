# The Bus Shelter

## Description

Concrete and timber, a bench, a tin timetable board, a single-pitch roof, at the
edge of the village where the track from the villa meets the road.

Repainted by the council in episode 2 in a yellow that is very slightly too green.

## Why It Matters

Elaya's first errand on screen, and the one that establishes her whole method:
she does it at night, she does it correctly, she never says it was her, and the
village concludes the council must have come back.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.9. The wrong yellow must be *almost* right. Frame it
against the correct faded Schönbrunn yellow of an older wall so the eye can catch
the mismatch without being told.
