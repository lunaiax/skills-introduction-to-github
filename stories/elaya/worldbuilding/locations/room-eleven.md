# Room Eleven

## Description

Small, north-facing, and cold. Iron bedstead, marble-topped washstand, one hard
chair, a closed stove that has not been lit since before the funeral, and a bell
rope beside the bed. The window looks onto grey rock rather than a view.

Nobody asks for eleven. People ask for the corner room with the mountain in it,
or for whatever is cheapest.

## Why It Matters

Havel asks for it by number in episode 1, which is how you ask for a room you
have stayed in before. The one live bell in the house answers this room, because
Aunt Mareau rewired it to — and never said why.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.6. Stove unlit until Elaya lights it at 3am in episode
1. The room may look better after that and must never look warm.
