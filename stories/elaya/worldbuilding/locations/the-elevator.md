# The Elevator

## Description

Shell pink with gilded mouldings, oval door panels, fluted glass inserts, gold
handles. A brass floor indicator above the doors with round enamel buttons: G, 1,
2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## Why It Matters

It works perfectly and Elaya almost never uses it. She takes the stairs, in a
hotel with a functioning lift, because the lift is for guests.

That is the character in one object, and it wants no dialogue at all.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.9. Floors G through 8, in that order, every time. Shot
dead square on.
