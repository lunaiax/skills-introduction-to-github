# The Winter Garden

## Description

A long glazed Art Nouveau conservatory down the eastern flank: curved iron ribs
and glass, palms and ferns in glazed pots, cane furniture with rose cushions, a
chequered tile floor, pink blinds half drawn.

Warm, humid, green.

## Why It Matters

The only warm green room in the series, and the only place in the hotel that is
visibly *alive*. Elaya waters it daily — the one piece of care she performs that
is not for a guest.

Used in episodes 6 and 9.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.14. Warmer and softer than any other interior. When
Elaya is here she is not camouflaged, because nothing in the room is her
colours.
