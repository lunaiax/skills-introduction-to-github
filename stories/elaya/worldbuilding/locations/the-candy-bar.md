# The Candy Bar

## Description

Rose pink and powder blue. A curved bar counter faced in glazed blue tile with a
pink marble top, gold-framed back-bar shelves, pink velvet stools on brass legs, a
chinoiserie mural of palms and pavilions, and pendant lamps with pink
ostrich-feather shades.

The way in is a sage green door with "Candy Bar" in gold script, half hidden by a
raspberry velvet curtain with ball fringe.

## Why It Matters

Aunt Mareau's joke: a cocktail bar named for a sweet shop, in a spa hotel where
the clientele were forbidden both. It has been fully stocked and never once busy.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.8. The green door and the raspberry curtain are its
identifying pair — one or both in frame whenever the bar is.
