# The Post Office

## Description

A wooden counter with a brass grille, a wall of numbered pigeonholes, a scale,
string, a franking stamp. Tobin's grey coat on a peg by the door, in every season.

The pigeonholes are sorted backwards — Friday on the left, Monday on the right —
because the end of the week is heavier and you want it done while you have the
arms for it.

## Why It Matters

The only interior in the series that is the right size for the people in it. It
should feel like relief after the villa, and that contrast is deliberate.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.8. Warmer than the villa by a fraction — still overcast,
still no hard shadow, but the space closes in kindly rather than opening out.
