# The Forecourt

## Description

A vast pink paved forecourt with a stone fountain at its centre, a low balustrade,
and twin curved stairs descending over an arched undercroft to the terraced
gardens.

Built to receive forty arrivals at once. Receives none.

## Why It Matters

The most efficient statement the series has. A space designed entirely around the
arrival of people, kept spotless, with nobody arriving.

Should open at least three episodes.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.13. Dead symmetrical, fountain on the centre line, no
cars and no luggage ever.
