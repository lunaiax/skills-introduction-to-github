# "Aunt Mareau's Room"

## Description

A closed door at the end of the second-floor corridor. Panelled, off-white, brass
handle, no number. Its bell is the only bell in the house that does not work.

Elaya has not opened it since the funeral.

## Why It Matters

The series' held object. Approached in episodes 1 and 5, forced open in episode 8
by a stranger valuing the house for tax.

What is inside must be **ordinary** — not a shrine, not a secret, not a revelation.
An old woman's bedroom, tidy, with her things where she left them. The
disappointment is the point: Elaya has kept a door shut for a year over nothing
she could not have handled, which is exactly what the gloves are.

## Rules For The Camera

See `art-direction.md` §5.7. Rendered as a closed door only, until episode 8. The
interior is not designed before then.
