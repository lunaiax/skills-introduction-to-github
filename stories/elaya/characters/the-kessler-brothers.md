# The Kessler Brothers

## Appearance

Sixties. Identical twins who have spent eleven years working at not being
identical.

**Anton** grew a full grey beard. **Emil** shaved his head. Beyond that they have
the same long nose, the same heavy eyelids, the same way of standing with the
weight on one hip, and — this is the thing — **the same hands.**

Neither has managed to alter his hands.

## Wardrobe

Both in working clothes, and this is the tell: **one is always in blue, the other
always in brown.** Anton blue, Emil brown. Moleskin trousers, collarless shirts,
waistcoats, flat caps, heavy boots.

Neither can remember deciding this. It happened at some point in the eleven years
and now it is simply true, and they would each rather die than be the one to
change.

## The detail

**In episode 5, the shot is their hands.** Two pairs, identical, doing the same
thing at the same speed at opposite ends of a village, for eleven years.

Elaya reconciles them with a broken clock and a lie of omission that is, examined
closely, quite cruel. It works.

## Description

Sixties, twins, have not spoken since their mother's funeral eleven years ago.
Neither can now remember the reason with any confidence, and both would rather
die than be the one to say so.

## Function

Episode 5, and the high-water mark of Elaya's powers: she reconciles them using a
broken clock and a lie of omission that is, examined closely, quite cruel.

It works. It is also the episode where Tobin catches her, and the audience sees
for the first time what she is actually willing to do to people for their own
good.
