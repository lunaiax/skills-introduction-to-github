# Hanno Krebs

## Appearance

Twenty-eight. Broad, gentle, slow-moving, with the permanently apologetic posture
of a man who has been told all his life that he is too big for indoors. Reddish
hair. A good open face that has recently learned it can be looked at with pity.

## Wardrobe

His wedding suit. Still. Nine days later, brushed every morning, worn every day,
because he packed nothing else and has not admitted to himself that he is
travelling.

## The case

He arrives on day nine of Mila's stay, having traced her by asking at every bus
stop in the valley, and he is not angry. That is the difficulty. **He wants to
apologise**, and he cannot say what for, and he is prepared to stand in the
forecourt until she comes down.

Elaya has to decide whether to tell him she is here.

She does not. She tells him one true fact instead — that the ten-past-four bus is
the only one that leaves the valley — and lets him work out the rest, which he
does, three days late and one bus behind. Whether that was mercy or cowardice is
the episode's open question.

## Function

The half of the case that makes it a moral problem instead of a puzzle. Elaya
solves what is wrong with Mila within four days and then spends the rest of the
episode deciding what to do about a man who has done nothing wrong at all.
