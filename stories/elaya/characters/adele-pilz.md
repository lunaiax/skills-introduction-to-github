# Adele Pilz

## Appearance

Sixty. Widowed eleven years. Round, pink, cheerful in the effortful way of
someone who has decided that cheerfulness is a duty. Talks to everyone. Talks
to Doro through the kitchen hatch for forty minutes at a stretch.

## Wardrobe

Home-made and carefully done: a floral cotton dress, a hand-knitted lilac
cardigan with a paper handkerchief in the sleeve, a felt hat with a pheasant
feather she has re-trimmed for three seasons.

She comes to the valley every year for eleven days. She has come for eleven
years. It is the only holiday she takes and she saves for it all year.

## Function

**The cost of episode 5.**

She is entirely innocent, entirely harmless, and entirely disposable — which is
why Elaya chooses her. The gossip lands. Frau Oberst's committee redirects
itself. Adele is asked, kindly, to shorten her stay, and does not understand why,
and is too polite to ask twice.

The last shot of episode 5 is Adele at the bus shelter with her case, eleven days
early, waving at nobody, and Elaya watching from the top of the drive and not
going down.

**She does not come back the following year.** The series never mentions it
again. Her name stays in the Register, in Elaya's hand, eleven times.
