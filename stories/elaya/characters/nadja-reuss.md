# Nadja Reuss

## Appearance

Thirties. Dark hair kept well, cut expensively to look uncomplicated. Fine-boned,
tired. She cries in the mornings, so at breakfast her face is faintly swollen and
by lunch it is composed — and Elaya notices the *order* of that, not the crying.

## Wardrobe

Excellent, understated, and wrong for the place. A camel cashmere coat too light
for mountain evenings. Silk scarves in muted colours, one per day. Narrow town
shoes. A gold wedding band and a good engagement ring.

She packed for a holiday. She came to make a decision. The luggage still has the
paper tags from the wedding trip eleven months ago, and she has not taken them
off, and Elaya cannot tell whether that is sentiment or inertia and neither can
she.

## The detail

**The ring.** She takes it off to swim in episode 6, sets it on the tiles, swims
alone in a pool nobody uses, and puts it back on afterwards.

Elaya sees this and does nothing. That is the episode.

## Description

Thirty-four, and **married eleven months.** Comes to the hotel for a week to
decide whether to leave her husband. Cries in the mornings, which is the wrong
time and is the detail Elaya notices.

Eleven months is the number that makes the episode hurt: she is still, by every
outward measure, a new wife. There is confetti in the lining of her good case.
She finds a piece of it on the second day and puts it in the ashtray.

## Function

**The counter-episode.** Elaya reads her, builds an errand for her, and then —
for reasons she cannot explain to herself — does not perform it.

Nadja decides anyway.

This is the hinge of the series: Elaya's interventions were never the mechanism.
People were always going to do what they were going to do. Everything she has
built her life on is, possibly, decoration.

She swims in the pool alone, which is the only time in ten episodes anyone uses
it.
