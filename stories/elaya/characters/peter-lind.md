# Peter Lind

## Appearance

Eleven, and small for it. Sharp-featured, permanently a little grubby about the
knees and cuffs. Hair cut at home by someone in a hurry. One front tooth chipped
at a corner.

Watchful in the way children are when they are somewhere they should not be.

## Wardrobe

At night, which is when we see him: striped flannel pyjamas under a man's tweed
coat four sizes too big, sleeves turned back in thick cuffs. Green rubber
wellingtons, occasionally on the wrong feet.

By day, in episode 9: a grey school jersey with a darned elbow and short trousers.

## The detail

**He carries a torch and never switches it on.** He is not investigating anything.
He is simply awake at three in the morning, as children sometimes are, and he
happens to be looking at the bus shelter when a woman in a ball gown starts
painting it.

## Description

Eleven. Village boy. Out at night when he should not be.

## Function

In episode 2 he sees Elaya repainting the bus shelter at three in the morning, in
a cream ball gown and pink gloves, alone, by lamplight.

He tells everyone. Nobody believes him.

He becomes the valley's small persistent ghost story — *the woman who paints things
at night* — and that legend runs quietly under the rest of the series. In episode
9 the village repaints a shutter for her, and it is Peter who organises it, because
he was the only one who ever knew.
