# Peter Lind

## Description

Eleven. Village boy. Out at night when he should not be.

## Function

In episode 2 he sees Elaya repainting the bus shelter at three in the morning, in
a cream ball gown and pink gloves, alone, by lamplight.

He tells everyone. Nobody believes him.

He becomes the valley's small persistent ghost story — *the woman who paints things
at night* — and that legend runs quietly under the rest of the series. In episode
9 the village repaints a shutter for her, and it is Peter who organises it, because
he was the only one who ever knew.
