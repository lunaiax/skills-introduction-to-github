# Otto Sanne

## Appearance

Twenty-six. Sandy, freckled, enormous ears he has clearly been teased about since
school and has stopped minding. He laughs at everything, including things that are
not jokes, which is how a nervous man sounds when he is happy.

## Wardrobe

A brand-new blazer with the shop's basting thread still in one shoulder seam,
which Elaya notices on day one and does not remove, because removing it would be
a message.

Sandals with socks. A camera he does not know how to load.

## The case

**Married nine days.** Room fourteen, which is the only room on that floor with
its own bathroom, which is why the hotel gave it to them.

They do not come out. Not in an unwholesome way — they come down for meals,
beaming, holding hands under the tablecloth — but between meals the door to
fourteen is shut, and there is a chair against it, and the Sannes have made it
extremely clear that they are on honeymoon and the world can wait nine days.

Which is a problem, because Anselm Ruck requires access to every room in the
building by Friday, and because the bottom left drawer contains a silver
propelling pencil labelled **Sanne, 1949** — taken from Otto's father, who stayed
here once, and who died two years ago, and whose son does not know the hotel is
the last place he slept.

## Function

Episode 8's honeymoon: the obstacle, the comedy, and the wound.

Elaya has to get into room fourteen, get the pencil onto Otto's washstand without
being seen, and keep Ruck out of that corridor for two more days — all of which
she does, and one of which she should not have.
