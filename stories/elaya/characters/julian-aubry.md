# Julian Aubry

## Appearance

Twenty-nine. Dark, narrow, handsome in an unfinished way. He has a habit of
standing slightly too far from whoever he is speaking to, which he thinks is
politeness.

## Wardrobe

A good navy overcoat, worn open. A soft collar, no tie. One good pair of shoes,
resoled. He travels light and dresses like someone who owns four excellent things
and nothing else.

## The case

**He comes for one night, every year, on the sixteenth of October, and has done
for five years.** He does not sightsee. He eats dinner, walks to the meadow gate,
stands there, and leaves in the morning.

The Register has him five times in Elaya's hand and once, in 1934, in a hand
before her aunt's — because he was here as an infant, for two days, under a
different surname.

## Function

Half of episode 7's case. He does not know why he comes. He was told, once, at
sixteen, that this was the place, and was given no other fact, and has been
returning to a building instead of to a person because the building is the only
address he has.
