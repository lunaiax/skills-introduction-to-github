# Renate Sanne

## Appearance

Twenty-four. Practical, cheerful, quicker than her husband and careful never to
be quicker in public. Brown plait pinned up in the morning and down by four.

She is the one who books, tips, asks for the extra blanket and reads the bus
timetable. Otto believes he is doing these things.

## Wardrobe

Home-made and excellent: two cotton dresses she cut and sewed herself for this
trip, a cardigan, flat sandals. She has looked at Elaya's gloves for exactly as
long as everyone does and, unlike everyone, has worked out that they are old.

## The detail

**She is the only guest in ten episodes who catches Elaya at it.**

On the sixth night she comes down for a glass of water at two in the morning and
finds the door of the linen room open and Elaya inside with the drawer out and
forty labelled objects on the shelf.

She does not scream, or accuse, or run. She looks for about four seconds, says
*"Good night,"* and goes back upstairs.

She never mentions it again. Not to Otto, not to Ruck, not to Elaya. In the
morning she says good morning exactly as before.

## Function

The episode's real detonation, and it is completely silent. Elaya has spent eight
episodes being unseen, and a twenty-four-year-old on her honeymoon saw the whole
of it in four seconds and simply decided it was not her business.

**Being forgiven by a stranger who owed her nothing is worse than being caught.**
It goes straight into episode 9.

Elaya puts the pencil on Otto's washstand the next night anyway. Renate finds it
first, and knows exactly where it came from, and hands it to her husband and says
nothing.
