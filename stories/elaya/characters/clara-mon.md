# Clara Mon

## Appearance

Fifty-eight. Small, elegant, extremely contained. Silver hair in a low twist.
She holds her own left wrist when she is not doing anything with her hands.

She has the same way of standing too far off as Julian does, and Elaya sees it
across a hall before she has any other evidence at all, and that is the moment
the episode turns.

## Wardrobe

Charcoal and pearl grey, beautifully cut, twenty years old and maintained. A
narrow black patent belt. Low court shoes. One pearl on a fine chain.

Nothing she wears has ever been fashionable, which is a decision.

## The case

First visit. Books four nights, arrives with a return ticket for two.

In 1934 she stayed here for two days with an infant and left with none, and the
Register records it because the Register records everything, including weather
and the number in the party on arrival and on departure.

## Function

The other half of episode 7. Elaya solves it on the second evening and then
spends the rest of the episode building the most elaborate errand of the series:
a dinner for two strangers, a seating plan, a bottle in the right year, a piece
of music, a power cut at nine.

It works. They talk for four hours.

**She watches the whole thing from the garden, through glass, in the rain.**

And Tobin, who has been waiting all episode for one sentence from her, waits in
the forecourt until eleven and then walks home.
