# Iris Melle

## Appearance

Thirty-one, and twenty-three years married to nobody's surprise but her own.
Very fair, very still, with the composure of someone who has learned that being
decorative is a job with hours.

## Wardrobe

Beautiful and chosen by him: a peach silk day dress, a fur stole too warm for
indoors and worn indoors anyway, seamed stockings, kitten heels on gravel. She
changes three times a day.

**And she does not own the brooch.** She has never been told what it is worth,
and she says so, once, mildly, and nobody in the room registers it except Elaya.

## Function

The reason Elaya lets Augustin leave without exposing him: the fraud collapses
onto Iris, not onto him, and Elaya works that out before she works out the fraud.

She is the episode's quiet second case, unsolved on purpose. Elaya does one small
thing for her — the fur is returned to her room brushed and re-lined, which is a
kindness with no message attached — and that is all anyone can do.
