# Iris Melle

## Appearance

Thirty-one, and married six weeks. Very fair, very still, with the composure of
someone who worked in an office for eleven years and has not yet worked out what
the new job is.

She is on her honeymoon and she is bored, and she has not admitted the second
part to herself, and Elaya watches her admit it in stages over eight days.

## Wardrobe

New. All of it, and all chosen by him: a peach silk day dress, a fur stole too
warm for indoors and worn indoors anyway, seamed stockings, kitten heels on
gravel. She changes three times a day because there are three days' worth of new
clothes a day.

**The wedding ring is loose.** She turns it back onto her knuckle roughly forty
times an hour and has stopped noticing.

**And she does not own the brooch.** She has never been told what it is worth,
and she says so, once, mildly, and nobody in the room registers it except Elaya.

## Function

The reason Elaya lets Augustin leave without exposing him: **the fraud collapses
onto Iris, not onto him.** Six weeks married, no money of her own, and a name she
has had for six weeks that is about to be in a newspaper. Elaya works that out
before she works out the fraud.

She is the episode's second case, and the interference is deliberate and small:
the fur comes back brushed and re-lined, and — this is the part Elaya will not
examine — the ring comes back resized. Two nights, the village jeweller, paid in
cash, put back on the washstand.

Iris finds it fits. She assumes her husband did it. She is warmer to him for two
days.

**Elaya has just improved a marriage she knows to be a trap, using her own money,
without being asked.** The series does not comment.
