# Anselm Ruck

## Appearance

Fifties. Neat, unremarkable, and possessed of a face nobody can describe
afterwards. Thinning brown hair combed flat. Rimless spectacles. Very clean, very
short fingernails.

He is not cold. He is *pleasant*, which is much worse.

## Wardrobe

A grey suit with no opinion in it, a white shirt, a plain dark tie. Black lace-up
shoes. A brown leather document case with a brass clasp. A fountain pen he uncaps
with two hands and caps again between entries.

A soft grey hat, removed indoors and held.

## The detail

**He holds his hat while he prices her life.** He is courteous throughout, thanks
her for each room, and never once raises his voice.

He requires access to every room in the building. That is not a threat; it is the
form. He is a man doing a job correctly, and that is what forces the door at the
end of the corridor.

## Description

Fifties. Tax assessor. Precise, unhurried, entirely without cruelty, and therefore
much worse than a villain.

## Function

In episode 8 he walks the hotel room by room, listing objects aloud and assigning
each a value, while Elaya follows him with the inventory she has kept since she
was a child and hears her whole life priced.

He requires access to every room. Including the one at the end of the corridor.

He is not the antagonist. He is a man doing a job correctly, which is what forces
the door — and that is worse than if he had wanted to.
