# Mila Vesth

## Appearance

Twenty-three. Small, dark, sunburnt across the nose in a way that says she was
outdoors somewhere much warmer than this a week ago. Bitten nails, kept out of
sight. Hair cut very short and very recently, by herself, in a mirror, in a hurry
— it is uneven at the back and she cannot see that it is.

## Wardrobe

Three days of clothes worn in rotation for two weeks, laundered nightly in the
basin: a navy skirt, two blouses, a man's grey pullover that is not hers. Canvas
shoes, wrong for the mountain. **No coat.**

And a large tan leather suitcase, good quality, brass-locked, which she carries
up herself and never opens.

## The case

She books for two weeks and pays in advance in cash, which nobody has done since
1958. She never unpacks. Every afternoon at ten past four she walks down to the
bus shelter, sits, watches the bus arrive, does not get on, and walks back.

**Fourteen days. Fourteen buses. She boards none of them.**

The suitcase contains one thing: a wedding dress, worn once, for forty minutes.

## Function

Episode 2's case and the series' first proper puzzle: Elaya solves a guest from
objects rather than confession. The dress is not evidence of heartbreak — Mila
was not left. **Mila left**, at the church door, and has been unable to go
forward or back ever since, because the suitcase makes the decision permanent
and unopened.

Elaya picks the lock, takes the dress, and has Doro cut it down into something a
person could wear on a bus. It comes back to the room without a note.

On the fifteenth day Mila boards.
