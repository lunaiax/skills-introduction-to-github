# Katrin Vogel

## Appearance

Twenty-two. Village girl, works at the dairy, strong forearms and a laugh that
carries across the forecourt. Freckled. Not composed and has never tried to be,
which makes her the exact opposite of every guest in the series so far.

## Wardrobe

For nine episodes we have not seen her. In episode 10: a plain white cotton dress
made by her mother, and a wreath of meadow flowers picked that morning at the
gate — the same field Elaya presses flowers from.

## The case

**There is no case, and that is the whole point.**

The church roof is off for the summer. There is nowhere in the valley to be
married that will hold sixty people and keep the rain out.

So Katrin Vogel walks up the drive in October and **asks.**

Out loud. To Elaya's face. For a specific thing, naming it, with a date, and
offers to pay what she can.

## Function

The inversion of the entire method, and the last beat of the series' argument.

Nine episodes of Elaya working out what people will not ask for. Then somebody
asks. It takes eleven seconds and it works.

Elaya arranges the wedding — the hall, the painted sky, the forty places finally
laid for forty people, the lift running, a room for the aunts — and for the first
time in her life the work is **commissioned, credited, thanked for by name, and
paid for.**

She hates roughly a third of that and the series lets you see which third.
