# Emil Roth

## Appearance

Twenty-four. Works on the upper farms — one of the four Tobin walks the post to
on foot. Sunburnt to the collar and white below it. Enormous hands he does not
know where to put.

## Wardrobe

His father's suit, taken in at the waist by Katrin's mother, and a shirt collar
that is a size too big and will be all day.

## The detail

**He is the only person in the valley who has spoken to Tobin every week for six
years** — because Tobin brings the post to his farm on foot, and Emil walks the
last half-mile with him, and they talk about weather and nothing, and neither
would call it a friendship.

Emil is the one who tells Tobin the wedding is at the hotel. He says it as a fact
about a building. He watches Tobin receive it and does not understand what he has
just watched.

## Function

He gets Tobin into the last episode wearing something other than the grey coat,
standing in a room full of people, with a reason to be there that is not a
mailbag.
