# Havel

## Description

Sixties, good coat, wrong shoes, one bag. Arrives in episode 1 and asks for room
eleven by number.

## Why Room Eleven

He stayed in it in 1961, with his wife, for six days. She has been dead four
years. He has not said any of this aloud and does not intend to.

## Function

The series' first errand and its template: he wants something he will not ask for,
Elaya works it out from evidence he did not mean to give, and she provides it
without ever letting him know she understood.

Returns in episode 10, briefly, and walks around the gate.
