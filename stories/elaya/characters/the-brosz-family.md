# The Brosz Family

## Who they are

A father, a mother, and two children aged nine and six. They arrive by car in
episode 10 with three cases and a folding pram and a great deal of noise.

## Appearance

Nothing to solve. Sunburn, sandals, a straw hat, a football. The mother is
pregnant and does not mention it and it is visible. The father cannot park.

## The case

**There is no case.**

They booked because the hotel was in a brochure. They stay four nights. They eat
enormously, use the pool, run in the corridors, break one gold-rimmed plate and
offer to pay for it, and leave a note at the desk thanking Doro by name.

Elaya reads their letters out of pure habit on the first night and finds a gas
bill and a postcard about a cousin's leg.

## Function

The last shot of the series' method. Elaya watches four people be entirely
knowable and entirely uninteresting and finds, to her surprise, that she has no
work to do — and that the building sounds different with them in it.

They are the reason room seventeen gets a bell in the last five minutes.

They walk **around** the gate, because the youngest wants to, and nobody stops
him.
