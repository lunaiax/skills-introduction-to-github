# Aunt Mareau

## Presence

A year dead when the series opens, and the most active character in it.

She is never seen alive — no flashbacks, ever. She exists entirely as objects she
altered: the Register in her hand, the one bell rewired to room eleven, the tab
with its number rubbed blank over years, the Candy Bar named as a joke, and a door
at the end of a corridor that has not opened since the funeral.

## Temperament, as deduced

Cheerful, secretive, and slightly frightening. She bought a failing hotel in a bad
year for a price she never disclosed and spent forty years failing to fill it
without ever once appearing to mind.

She taught a nine-year-old to forge her handwriting. Nobody has asked why.

## The Question She Left

Why the bell to eleven, and why rub out the number? Elaya has never asked it aloud
because there is nobody left to ask.

Answered in episode 10, together with the gloves, because they are the same
answer.
