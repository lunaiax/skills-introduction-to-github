# Stefan Lange

## Appearance

Thirty-eight and ill, and the illness is never named on screen. Thin at the
wrists. Sits down more often than a man his age should and always makes it look
like a decision.

Very funny, in a dry, low, unhurried way. He is the only guest in ten episodes
who makes Doro laugh, and she does not forgive him for it.

## Wardrobe

Soft old cashmere in fawn and dove grey, a scarf indoors, slippers by mid-evening.
A blanket over the knees in the winter garden that he refuses for nine days and
accepts on the tenth without comment.

## The detail

**He asks for the room with the best view and takes the one with the shortest
staircase.** Elaya reassigns him to the good room on the floor below without
being asked and without mentioning it, and he understands exactly what has
happened and says nothing, and that is the closest thing to a thank-you the
series permits before episode 9.

## Function

The reason the lie in episode 5 is defensible, which is what makes it dangerous.
