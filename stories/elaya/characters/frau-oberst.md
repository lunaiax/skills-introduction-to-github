# Frau Oberst

## Appearance

Sixties. Tall, very upright, handsome rather than pretty and entirely aware of the
distinction. Steel-grey hair set weekly and immovable. Spectacles on a fine gold
chain, taken off to look at people properly and put back on to look at paperwork.

She has excellent posture and uses it as an argument.

## Wardrobe

A good loden-green tweed suit, skirt to mid-calf, worn with a cream blouse buttoned
to the throat. A different brooch each episode, always small, always gold. Sensible
low-heeled brown shoes, polished. A structured handbag carried in the crook of the
arm, never on the shoulder.

**And gloves.** Short kid-leather gloves in fawn, wrist-length, correct.

## The detail

**She wears gloves too, and that is the joke.** Hers are short, plain and
absolutely proper. She has called Elaya's elbow-length pink ones "theatrical" in
public more than once — and she is the one person in the valley qualified to say
so, because she understands exactly what gloves are for and what they cost.

She is not wrong about anything except what it means.

## Description

Sixties. The village's self-appointed authority on what is proper. Runs the
committee that repaints things, badly.

## Function

The closest thing the series has to an antagonist, and deliberately a small one.
She disapproves of the hotel, of the girl in it, and of the gloves, which she has
called "theatrical" in public more than once.

She is not wrong about anything except what it means. Her instinct that something
is off at Villa Mareau is entirely correct.

In episode 8 it is her committee that triggers the tax valuation.
